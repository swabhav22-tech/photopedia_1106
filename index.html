Landscape & nature photography

What the light
touches first

A field journal of mountains, water and weather — shot on foot, mostly before sunrise.

Every frame here started with a walk in the dark and a guess about where the sun would land. Some mornings it works out. This is a running collection of the ones that did — organized loosely by what the land was doing that day, not by where I stood to shoot it.

Selected work

Behind the camera

I'm a self-taught landscape photographer chasing weather more than locations. Most of what's here was shot within a few hours of home — the point was never to travel far, just to show up often enough that the light eventually cooperated.

New work goes up on Instagram first, usually with the story of how the morning actually went. This site is the edited, slower version of that.
